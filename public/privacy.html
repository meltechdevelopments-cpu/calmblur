CalmBlur Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. 100% On-Device Caption & Scene Scanning

CalmBlur scans closed captions ([CC]) and video playback timestamps locally inside your own browser or phone. Your streaming history, subtitles, account credentials, and video frames are never uploaded, recorded, or transmitted to any server.

2. Subscription Pass Key Validation

When you enter a CalmBlur Pass key, your browser sends only the license key string to Polar.sh (https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys/validate) to verify that your subscription is active. Payment details are handled directly by Polar.sh as Merchant of Record and are never seen or stored by CalmBlur.

3. Local Preferences

Your chosen trigger categories, custom trigger words, and pass key status are saved locally on your device using standard browser storage (localStorage / storage.local).

4. Zero Analytics or Ad Trackers

CalmBlur includes zero third-party advertising trackers, cookies, or behavioural analytics scripts.
